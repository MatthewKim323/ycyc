# Storylines (2026-11-15)

The one thing: connect an app once, and every AI you use can read it and act in it.
In the viewer's words: "ycyc let Claude log my breakfast in MyFitnessPal."
Carrier: the app's own example prompts, "log two eggs and toast for breakfast" (MyFitnessPal) and "what did I bench last week?" (Strong).
Copy from the app, used verbatim: "Your apps, in every AI." / "Connect apps that have no API." / "Set it up once." / "One link. Add it once to every assistant you use."

Constraints from research: product on screen by 0:02 (real screens beat animated ones, 0:08 vs 0:20). Aim for 60-80 s. VO at ~130 wpm (~140 words). Say "Capy", "Revyl" and "Convex" out loud, since the vibeapps AI judge reads YouTube captions. Unlisted YouTube with captions on. Show one full loop on a real device: connect, AI writes, tap to approve, data lands. Only claims that are true today.

## A. Two eggs and toast (recommended: the full loop a judge can follow, on real screens)

One object across apps, told as an object-to-object chain: the sentence "log two eggs and toast" travels from a Claude chat into ycyc, into MyFitnessPal and back. Each shot ends on the thing that opens the next one.

- **First 2 s:** real iPhone, Claude chat, macro. "log two eggs and toast for breakfast" is sent and Claude replies "I can't access MyFitnessPal." The reply bubble shrinks into a tap target.
- **Beats:** the wall (Claude can't reach MFP) → reveal: the ycyc mark on the dot wave, "Your apps, in every AI." → pick MyFitnessPal → sign in on its own page → watch it build (Signed in, Reading your account, Writing tools, Testing a real request) → copy one link → paste it into Claude → same prompt, Claude logs it → the approval card on the phone, one tap → payoff: the row lands in the MFP diary, then in ChatGPT, "what did I bench last week?" answers from Strong with no setup → claim: "Set it up once." plus "Built today with Capy, Revyl and Convex" → end card: the ycyc square slides into the app glyph like the connect-sheet lockup, "Your apps, in every AI.", repo URL.
- **Look:** the app's black with the silk dot wave as the home stage. Real phone captures at 1.6-2x, pushes that follow each tap, Inter, white only.
- **Sound:** sparse VO, soft electronic bed, UI ticks on taps, the one big hit on the approve tap, ending by subtraction.

## B. It can't log your lunch (type-led, cut on the beat, needs the fewest captures)

Type carries the argument, then life before and after. Giant kinetic Inter on the dot wave cuts on the music, and real screens only arrive as proof.

- **First 2 s:** "Your AI can write code." in giant type, a real Claude reply flicking behind it, a hard cut on the beat.
- **Beats:** "Plan a trip." "Pass the bar." → suck-out → "It can't log your lunch." → a dark wall of app tiles (MyFitnessPal, Strong) marked with no API → "So ycyc builds one." → the four build steps at macro scale, one per beat → "One link." fans out to Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Muse → three real proof cuts (log, approve, bench answer) → sponsor line → end card.
- **Look:** type fills 60% of the frame. The shader pulses on the downbeats, and real screens appear only in the proof.
- **Sound:** music-led. The VO speaks only the punchlines.

## C. Through the link (bold: one continuous take)

The object is the explanation, told as an endless zoom: the link is a portal and the camera never cuts.

- **First 2 s:** the dot wave fills the frame. The camera dives at one bright dot, and it resolves into the link icon.
- **Beats:** through the link into Claude's chat → through the tool call into the ycyc build steps → through the approval card into the MFP diary row → pull back out of the phone into the dot wave, where every connected app is a dot → through it again into ChatGPT, where the bench answer lands → pull all the way out to the end card.
- **Look:** a single camera on one ease family. Each world sits inside a detail of the last one.
- **Sound:** a continuous riser-and-release bed with the VO over it, and no hard hits until the end card.
