Two decisions before 10am

1. Which app do we connect live on stage?

Owner: matt and teamNeeded by: kickoff, 9am
A. MyFitnessPal, web route
  1. In Claude: "log my Chipotle bowl"
  2. Connect screen pops, log in to MyFitnessPal
  3. Entry shows up in the diary
Pro
200M+ users. Only ChatGPT Health has it, so Claude, Grok and Muse users have nothing. Community servers already prove read and write work.
Con
Looks like a normal integration. Doesn't prove we can do apps with no web version.
B. Strong, cloud phone
  1. In Claude: "log push day, bench 185 for 5x5"
  2. A live Revyl phone opens Strong and taps it in
  3. The room watches it happen
Pro
Phone-only app, no API, no web. Nobody else can do this, MoChiPi and Rube included. Most visual.
Con
Untested: third-party APK install, login on an emulator, agent taps run about 15s per step.
C. Both, one storyRecommended
  1. Breakfast logged to MyFitnessPal (A)
  2. Workout logged in Strong on the cloud phone (B)
  3. Switch to ChatGPT: "what did I eat and lift today?" works with no setup
Pro
A is the safety net if the cloud phone flakes live. B is the moment people remember.
Con
Two paths in 8 hours. Build A first, done by 2pm, then B.

Why C: the demo has to answer "isn't this just MoChiPi?" without a slide. Strong on a cloud phone answers it. MyFitnessPal keeps the demo alive if the emulator misbehaves.

2. Which category leads the pitch?

Owner: mattChanges: the first line of the pitch and which apps get built after tomorrow
Health and fitness loggingRecommended

Biggest dark spots in the map: Flo (about 80M monthly users), MyFitnessPal, Strong, Garmin, Cal AI. Logged daily, and Apple Health only carries summaries, so sets, reps, foods and symptoms are stuck in each app.

Students

Relatable, and Gradescope, Piazza and Blackboard have no student API. But schools are blocking Canvas tokens because of AI doing assignments, so it reads as a cheating tool.

Everyday ordering

Chipotle, ParkMobile, Lyft are open. But Gemini already drives 20 US apps and ChatGPT has Starbucks, DoorDash, Target. That window is closing fast.
